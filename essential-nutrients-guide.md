# 🌱 Daily Intake: What Determines It?

The "recommended daily intake" is not the same for everyone. It depends on several factors:

Factor	Effect on Daily Need
Age	Children, adults, and older adults have different requirements.
Sex	Men and women differ for nutrients like iron and magnesium.
Pregnancy	Increased need for folate, iron, iodine, calcium, and others.
Breastfeeding	Higher needs for many vitamins and minerals.
Body weight	Protein needs are based on body weight (g/kg).
Physical activity	Athletes may need more protein, magnesium, and calories.
Medical conditions	Some diseases affect absorption or increase requirements.
Diet pattern	Vegetarians/vegans may need to pay closer attention to B12, iron, zinc, and omega-3.

  📱 Recommended "Daily Nutrition Checklist" for Your App
A user who eats most of these in a day will cover the majority of essential nutrients:
* 🥣 Oats or millet
* 🫘 1–2 servings of dal/beans
* 🥛 2 servings of dairy (or fortified alternatives)
* 🥬 2 cups leafy green vegetables
* 🥕 1 orange/yellow vegetable (carrot or sweet potato)
* 🍅 2–3 other vegetables
* 🍎 2 fruits (including one high in vitamin C, like amla or guava)
* 🥜 A handful of mixed nuts
* 🌱 1–2 tbsp mixed seeds (flax, chia, pumpkin, sesame)
* 🫒 Healthy cooking oil in moderation
This pattern provides good coverage of protein, fiber, healthy fats, vitamin A, B-complex, C, D (if fortified foods or adequate sunlight are included), E, K, calcium, iron, magnesium, potassium, zinc, iodine (from iodized salt), and omega-3, making it an excellent foundation for overall health.
